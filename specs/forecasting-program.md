## 1. The premise, answered

The question that started this: *"assume that given enough data, you should be able to
predict earthquakes."* The honest answer splits the premise in two.

SPLIT THE PREMISE. It contains two claims with opposite answers, and the part of it that says "given enough data" is the part the evidence specifically refutes.

**(1) Deterministic prediction — a time/place/magnitude window narrow enough for an authority to act on: NO, and data volume is not the binding constraint.** The arithmetic is the argument. The base rate for damaging shaking at a specific high-hazard site is ~1e-4/day (catalog-derived California: M>=6 = 0.836/yr, M>=6.5 = 0.387/yr, M>=7 = 0.143/yr on n=7, whose 95% Poisson interval is ~0.06-0.29/yr — a factor of 5, so do not over-read the third figure). Reaching p=0.5 for a named place and day requires a sustained ~5,000x probability gain. The largest sustained gain ever demonstrated is ~1,000x, and only in the hours-to-days after a nearby M>=6 — i.e. forecasting the aftershocks of an earthquake that has already happened and is already in the catalog. The structural bar: standard ETAS draws magnitudes independently of history given the rate, so it cannot in principle distinguish a foreshock from a small mainshock. Helmstetter & Sornette's prediction-gain ceiling (~21 at 1% alarm fraction for M>=6, degrading fast as you demand more targets) follows from that assumption plus a branching ratio — it is conditional, not an unconditional bound, and relaxing magnitude-independence is the one place a genuine breakthrough could live. That is exactly where a century of work has produced no prospective skill: Parkfield's pre-registered 95% window closed in 1993 against a 2004 earthquake, under the densest precursor instrumentation ever fielded, recording nothing diagnostic; Japan formally abandoned the statutory Tokai strainmeter prediction framework in 2017 after ~40 years; the b-value Foreshock Traffic Light System has, seven years on, no CSEP registration, no Molchan trajectory, no ROC, no probability gain and no false-alarm rate, and its famous "95% correct on 58 sequences" is consistent with *zero information* because ~5-15% of mainshocks are followed by something larger, so a constant "green" classifier already scores 85-95%.

**(2) Probabilistic forecasting — YES, measurably, and it already works.** First-party measurement, reproduced from files on disk this session (ComCat California M>=2.5, EarthquakeNPP frozen splits; fit 1981-2007 on 55,442 targets, scored 2007-01-01..2020-01-17 on 21,889 targets over 959,823 km^2): ETAS beats a space-time-homogeneous Poisson by **6.006 nats/event** (nll 7.2554 vs 13.2619), decomposing as +5.085 spatial and +0.922 temporal.

**(3) But the field's own decomposition of that skill is backwards, and correcting it changes what to build.** The shipped ETAS fit has `free_background=false` — mu is a single global constant, 4.645e-07/km^2/day. There is no smoothed-seismicity map anywhere in it. So the +5.085 "spatial" term is the *triggering kernel expressed in space*, not a static "where" map. Building the static map ourselves (adaptive-kNN Gaussian KDE, same 55,442 training events, same 21,889 test events, 0.1% water level) gives **+1.289 nats at k=2, +1.378 at k=4, +1.423 at k=8** — a static map recovers only 25-28% of ETAS's spatial term. A second, independent grid-KDE estimate in Design 2 reaches +1.71, and an in-sample *oracle* map fitted to the test events themselves reaches only +3.98, i.e. ETAS's spatial term is unreachable by any static map. The honest split on this dataset is roughly **1.3-1.7 nats of "earthquakes happen where earthquakes happened" and 4.3-4.7 nats of clustering** — the inverse of the circulating "~5 nats is where, ~1 nat is when" framing, which was computed against a spatially uniform ETAS background. The thing that works is Omori-Utsu triggering, and it works better than the literature's headline decomposition credits it. (Note: this split is a function of Mc, horizon and baseline, not a constant, and it shrinks at higher target magnitude.)

**(4) Why "enough data" is the wrong frame — the constraint is target count, not disk.** In the same 13.05-year window, the best-instrumented region on Earth produced: 21,889 events at M>=2.5, 7,007 at M>=3.0, **727 at M>=4.0, 62 at M>=4.95, 6 at M>=5.95**. Catalog volume is enormous and growing — template matching took southern California from ~180k to 1.81M events over 2008-2017, Mc 1.7 -> 0.3 — and no precursor emerged from the denser catalog. None of that manufactures large-earthquake targets, and the targets are not independent: 21 of 24 CSEP-Italy decade targets came from two aftershock sequences, five of ten years contained zero targets, and 74% of the winning model's entire margin accrued in one 2012 window. Do the power arithmetic before fitting anything: with per-event log-ratio dispersion sigma (to be *measured* on dev, not assumed), minimum detectable IGPE at 80% power is ~2.8*sigma/sqrt(N_eff). At sigma ~ 1 nat: M>=4.95 with 62 clustered targets (N_eff ~25 sequences) gives MDE ~0.56 nats; M>=4.0 with 727 targets (N_eff ~175) gives ~0.21; only M>=2.5 falls to ~0.06-0.07. The largest credible margin anyone has ever demonstrated over ETAS is **~0.1 bits = 0.069 nats** (FERN+, unreplicated, never CSEP-registered). **We are underpowered by roughly 8x at the threshold that matters, and the only magnitude band where we can detect the largest improvement anyone has ever plausibly claimed is the band where the improvement has no hazard consequence.** More sensors produce more small events, not more large targets. The binding constraints are the Gutenberg-Richter tail and the calendar.

**(5) The one way the premise IS right, and it is untested.** More data does not give more *targets*, but it gives more *training* events — better-constrained kernels and a spatially resolved background. HKJ's prospective win over Frankel is widely attributed to smoothing a *lower-Mc catalog*, not to a cleverer kernel, and nobody has cleanly ablated that. Smoothing at Mc 4.0/3.0/2.0/1.5/1.0 while holding the forecast target at M>=4.95, with a network-density completeness control, is the highest-expected-value cheap experiment in the field and it is Stage 1 of this program. That is the honest steelman of "given enough data," and it is worth a real shot.

**(6) Two decades of demonstrated statistical skill have produced ZERO demonstrated decision utility.** Not one clustering model has been shown, in any cost-loss framework, to produce net benefit for a real decision. A 10x gain on 1e-4/day is 1e-3/day: 0.7% over a week, a 999:1 false-alarm ratio. That clears break-even for cheap preparedness (C/L ~1e-5 to 1e-4) by 10-100x, is marginal at C/L ~1e-3 (closing one deficient building), and fails evacuation (C/L ~7e-3) by roughly an order of magnitude. Only in the post-mainshock regime (1,000x -> ~10%/day, ~52%/week) do exclusion zones become defensible — and that regime is already served by 1989-vintage Reasenberg-Jones and Omori-Utsu statistics, not by anything we would build.

**(7) One correction to the field's own convention that this program must not repeat.** "ETAS is the benchmark" is a convention, not a measured result. Leading clustering models are generally within noise of each other; the CSEP t-test of Rhoades et al. (2011) has a mis-specified variance and non-uniform p-values under the null; independent implementations of nominally the same ETAS produce materially different forecasts; and the shipped uniform-background ETAS we inherited is very likely *weaker* than a properly configured one. Every published "X did not beat ETAS" result — and symmetrically every anti-challenger result — was scored against a movable bar. Fixing the bar is Stage 1, and fixing it may well eat the entire headroom this program was premised on. That would be a real finding, not a failure.

**NET, committed in advance:** expect **+0.0 to +0.1 nats over a properly-configured ETAS at M>=2.5-3.0, indistinguishable from zero at M>=4.0, and formally undecidable at M>=5** with available targets in one region. Build it anyway, because the harness, the corrected baseline, and the decision layer have value independent of whether the model wins — and because the field's single most reproducible finding is that retrospective gains do not survive prospective testing, which means the only way to know is to pre-register and look.

## 2. Architecture

**CORE DESIGN DECISION — the challenger is an OFFSET on ETAS, never a replacement.** All three designs converged on this independently; it is architectural, not stylistic.

    log lambda_model(t,x,m) = log lambda_B2(t,x,m) + SUM_k beta_k * f_k(z(t,x)),   beta ~ Normal(0, tau^2 I)

beta = 0 reproduces the baseline *exactly*, byte-identically. This makes "no improvement" the default, gives a well-defined nested likelihood-ratio test, and structurally prevents the challenger from being credited with ETAS's 6 nats — which is precisely how the DeVries-class results and the pre-EarthquakeNPP NPP benchmark produced skill that later evaporated. tau is tuned on the development window only.

=== BASELINE LADDER — every rung is built, reported, and a mandatory reference ===

**B0 — space-time homogeneous Poisson.** sll = -13.7745 = -log(959,823 km^2), exactly the uniform density, which confirms LL units are per-km^2 and lets any number below be hand-checked. Diagnostic only. It is NEVER a headline baseline: it is the strawman that manufactures the field's "150-1000x" gains.

**B1 — time-independent adaptively-smoothed seismicity, HKJ-style.** Adaptive kNN Gaussian kernel (bandwidth = distance to k-th training neighbour, floored), normalised over the region polygon, with the uniform water-level weight treated as a TUNED parameter whose sensitivity is reported beside every score. Measured on disk: +1.289 (k=2) / +1.378 (k=4) / +1.423 (k=8) nats over B0. **Reconciliation task:** Design 2's grid-KDE estimate of the same quantity is +1.71. Stage 1 must reconcile the two estimators to within 0.05 nats or report the discrepancy as a first-class finding about how baseline-dependent these numbers are. Plus the ablation the literature never ran cleanly: smooth a LOWER-Mc catalog (QTM M>=1.0, WHITE M>=0.6) while holding the forecast target at M>=4.0-4.95, with a network-density completeness control.

**B2 — ETAS WITH SPATIALLY VARYING BACKGROUND, mu(x) = B1 map.** This is the real baseline and it does not exist in any artifact we inherited: `parameters_0.json` has `free_background=false` and a single global mu = 4.645e-07/km^2/day. EM inversion over (mu-scaling, K, a, c, omega, tau, d, gamma, rho) using the `etas` package (installs from git, not PyPI; its unconditional seismostats import needs seismostats pip-installed separately). **B2 is the number every claim in this program is measured against.**

**B2' — sequence-specific Bayesian Reasenberg-Jones with generic tectonic-regime priors (Page 2016).** The comparator USGS actually ships operationally. Comparing tuned ETAS to *generic* RJ is the unfair comparison the literature keeps making; we will not make it.

**B3 — ETAS upgrades the evidence actually supports, each individually ablatable.**
- B3a full number DISTRIBUTION, not mean rate: negative-binomial or branching-simulation counts. Cheap, strictly better under proper scoring, and the specific defense against one Ridgecrest-scale sequence destroying a decade of score. UCERF3-ETAS's documented failure mode was too little variability in the magnitude-number distribution.
- B3b short-term aftershock incompleteness: ETASI/PETAI with b-positive / a-positive magnitude-difference estimators and a time-resolved Mc(t), gated by a Lilliefors FMD-exponentiality test. The only place NPPs have ever demonstrably beaten ETAS. Also circular if ignored — parameters fit on catalogs incomplete in exactly the post-mainshock hours, propagated into forecasts, scored against a catalog with the same deficiency.
- B3c all-generations cascade branching simulation. Dominates where the local branching ratio n > 0.8 (Landers/Northridge/Hector Mine corridors), negligible elsewhere.
- B3d tapered / corner-magnitude GR head (regime-set corner magnitude via Bird & Kagan SHIFT / PB2002 class). ~0 at M>=5; material for the M>=8 tail where unbounded GR over-predicts, which is where any public-facing number lives.

**THREE INDEPENDENT ETAS IMPLEMENTATIONS** — `lmizrahi/etas` 3.0.0, `opensha-oaf`, R `SAPP`/`ETAS` — with a documented reconciliation of their parameter disagreement. The ETAS likelihood is badly non-identifiable, branching ratios near 1 destabilise simulation, and "X did not beat ETAS" is otherwise a claim about one group's tuning. A challenger must beat the **best** of the three. Staged for buildability: one implementation suffices for Stage 0/1 reproduction; all three are required before any Stage 3 claim.

=== THE CHALLENGER LAYER ===

f_theta is capacity-limited and escalates only on a pre-registered ladder, no skipping, each rung reported: **(i)** linear in standardised covariates with an L2 prior centred at 0 calibrated so the null is recovered under no signal; **(ii)** monotone-constrained gradient-boosted trees, depth <= 3; **(iii)** small GRU/attention sequence encoder, permitted ONLY on catalogs exceeding ~10^4 training events (RECAST's own stated gain threshold) and only after (i) and (ii) are scored.

**Covariates, ordered by evidence strength, with honest priors:**
1. mu(x) from B1 — enters through ETAS's background, never as a free feature (no double-counting).
2. Rupture-length-scaled anisotropic spatial kernel, oriented by focal mechanisms where GCMT moment tensors exist (40,514 events with full MT; 21,598 carry mb/MS/Mw simultaneously — a ready-made cross-scale calibration set). Werner et al. 2011 found this best of eight kernels; a neural encoder learning fault-aligned anisotropy gained ~4% IGPE. Prior: single-digit percent, not a step change. **Most likely non-null contributor in the whole stack.**
3. Tectonic-regime and sequence-specific productivity priors (Bayesian shrinkage generic -> sequence-specific as a sequence unfolds). Inputs verified: Bird PB2002 steps (5,819 boundary segments, 564 KB, the highest value-per-byte item in the inventory) and GEM Global Active Faults (13,696 traces; note `net_slip_rate` is a string tuple with 3,291 empty, so a naive moment-rate sum under-counts ~24%). Prior: improves CALIBRATION, not sharpness.
4. Foreshock-occurrence indicator (M>=M0 within 5 d and 10 km, M0 swept) plus the Reasenberg-Jones generic foreshock prior. Best-validated feature in the precursor class; Gentili's FORE model beat a b-value alarm model at every alarm rate. Needs only a standard Mc 2-3 catalog. **Build this before anything else in the precursor family.**
5. Ogata (1996) cluster-shape discriminants: inter-event time compactness, inter-epicentral distance, chronological magnitude-increase tendency. Out-of-sample validated in Japan (fit 1926-75, tested 1976-93). As ETAS residuals.
6. Rate RESIDUAL against ETAS, never raw rate or raw foreshock count. The "72% of mainshocks have foreshocks" figure collapses to ~18% unexplained once ETAS is the null. Measured as a residual or it re-derives ETAS.
7. Gated b-positive standardised deviation, (b_recent - b_bg)/sigma_b, NEVER a raw percentage, behind a hard gate refusing to emit when Mc(t) is unstable or the FMD fails exponentiality. Prior: ~0. The FTLS decision threshold sits at or below 1 sigma of a single window estimate once Aki's known underestimation and sliding-window autocorrelation are accounted for; the signal survives hand curation but not blind automation, and an operational system is by definition blind.
8. SSE/tremor state variable in subduction settings (keliankaz/global_SSE, ~1,000-1,200 SSEs across ~31 catalogs; Cascadia tremor through 2018, 62,310 detections). Up to ~3x relative effect, near-zero absolute PPV — Cascadia has produced 20+ ETS episodes and zero megathrusts. Ship only with absolute probability rendered beside the multiplier.
9. Geodetic strain rate (GSRM v2.1) as a multiplicative term on B1, long-horizon spatial only. Prior: near zero — the canonical case of real retrospective gain that did not replicate prospectively. Needs a human fetch; not on the critical path.

**NEGATIVE CONTROLS, first-class citizens run through the identical pipeline on every evaluation:**
- a random-phase surrogate covariate (same marginal distribution, destroyed temporal alignment);
- a leak canary: a deterministic hash of the event id, carrying zero physical information;
- accelerating moment release with per-event optimised radius and window — **excluded as a feature, retained as a leak detector.** It must score < 0.05 nats under the grouped forward-in-time splitter AND > 0.30 nats under a naive random k-fold, demonstrating the splitter measurably removes a known leak rather than merely being asserted to;
- tidal Coulomb stress phase, computable offline today with zero acquisition (pysolid/pyTMD verified: 7.23e-8 areal strain p2p at Parkfield, ~2.2 kPa, two implementations agreeing to 0.5%). Prior exactly zero; its job is pipeline canary. If ever used seriously, note that within 100-200 km of coast ocean tidal loading is comparable to and phase-shifted from the body tide, and omitting it can invert the sign of a reported correlation.

If any canary or surrogate scores above zero within bootstrap noise, the pipeline leaks and every result in that run is void. Non-negotiable, every evaluation.

=== ML, TIERED BY EVIDENTIARY STANDING (Design 2's framing, adopted) ===
- **Tier 1 detection: unconditional yes.** PhaseNet/EQTransformer + GaMMA + template matching over the open credential-free `scedc-pds` continuous-waveform archive to extend a QTM-class catalog past 2017 where QTM stops. This is a signal-processing win, not a forecasting claim. Budget for documented cross-domain degradation and for picker failure on overlapping arrivals during dense sequences — precisely when completeness matters most. Gated behind Stage 1 showing the Mc lever is real (see stages).
- **Tier 2 rate correction: allowed only as a low-capacity residual on a frozen ETAS offset.** Pre-registered expectation 0 to +0.1 nats.
- **Tier 3 independent-mainshock prediction: FORBIDDEN as a product claim.** Dozens of independent large events is not a supervised-learning label budget. Permitted only inside the Stage 5 falsification experiment.

=== MAGNITUDE HEAD — separate, quarantined module (Design 2's unique contribution) ===
Standard ETAS's P(m) independent of history is the structural ceiling. Stage 5 builds P(m | history) — conditioned on Mc-gated b-positive deviation, Ogata magnitude-increase tendency, foreshock cluster compactness, tectonic-regime flag — and scores it on exactly one question: *does the next event in a sequence exceed the largest so far?* That is the only question with prediction value, and it is the question the entire foreshock/b-value literature has never scored properly.

=== LEAKAGE AND PROVENANCE AS ARCHITECTURE, NOT HYGIENE ===
- Splitter groups by **rupture AND seismic zone AND enforces a forward-in-time cut**. No random k-fold, ever. Declustering (bruces: Gardner-Knopoff, Reasenberg, Zaliapin-Ben-Zion, validated against Trugman's own MATLAB Zaliapin implementation) supplies the grouping.
- **Vintage-correct catalogs.** Forecasts scored against the catalog *as it existed at issue time*, not the reprocessed final catalog — the error that forced New Zealand's CSEP results to be re-scored. The Shelter dual-clock feed (1,456,593 events, zero missing days across 1,166 dates, event_time + ingest_time + revision + tombstone, manifest sha256-verified) is the only public archive supporting this cleanly and is the most underused asset available. Read `manifest.json` first and honour the `archives[]`/`partitions[]` split — archived months are pruned from the data branch and a naive crawler silently gets 12% of the corpus with no error.
- **Fitting is offline; evaluation may be anywhere.** The ETAS likelihood surface is non-identifiable and branching ratios near 1 destabilise simulation, so parameter inversion never runs in an unaudited context. Fit emits a small frozen params file carrying fit window, assumed Mc, catalog SHA, branching ratio and git SHA; the evaluator refuses to run on a catalog whose Mc/SHA era does not match.

=== PRESENTATION CONTRACT (Design 3's, enforced by executable tests) ===
The forecast object is frozen and the renderer throws on anything else:
`{pAbsolute, pBaseline, gain, horizonDays, magThreshold, causeEvent|null, halfLifeDays, expiresAt, nEffective, paramsVersion}`
- `gain` is unrenderable without `pAbsolute` — "10x elevated" with no denominator is the single most misleading string this program could emit.
- `causeEvent: null` => **no elevated state may render at all.** An elevation without a nameable earthquake behind it is either a bug or a precursor claim, and we make neither.
- Absolute probability leads, in natural frequency ("about 1 chance in 250 over the next week"); the complement renders at equal type size; elevated states carry a decay clock and expire on the Omori half-life; pull-only (no push, no badge — a 1-in-250 delivered as an interrupt reads as an imperative); the baseline state never says "safe" (the L'Aquila failure was reassurance, not alarm) but states the normal rate for that place.
- An import-graph test asserts no module reachable from the model path imports any live/impact feed (alert levels, disaster-impact rows, sub-Mc anomaly feeds), and that every event entering the model passes a schema gate requiring finite magnitude on the catalog's declared scale.

## 3. Evaluation protocol (pre-registered)

**THE PROTOCOL IS WRITTEN, HASHED AND COMMITTED BEFORE ANY MODEL IS FIT.** A `seal.py` script records (a) the SHA of the pre-registration document, (b) the SHA of the model and harness code, (c) a UTC timestamp, into an append-only log. The held-out window cannot be read by any script unless a seal exists whose timestamp precedes the read, and CI fails if a results file's criterion SHA does not appear in git history at least one commit *earlier* than the results commit. Any result produced without a valid preceding seal is reported as exploratory and can never support a skill claim. This is the single mechanism that makes everything below non-gameable; without it the rest is decoration.

--- SPLITS (frozen, verbatim, decided now) ---
PRIMARY: ComCat California M>=2.5.
- auxiliary 1971-01-01 .. 1981-01-01 (history for triggering, never scored)
- fit 1981-01-01 .. 2007-01-01 (55,442 targets)
- DEV 2007-01-01 .. 2014-01-01 (all decisions, all tuning, unlimited looks)
- **HELD-OUT 2014-01-01 .. 2020-01-17 (opened exactly once per stage, after the seal)**
- RESERVE (single replacement window, used only if a protocol breach retires the above) 2020-01-18 .. 2026-01-01

*Contradiction resolved:* Design 1 proposed EarthquakeNPP-aligned splits; Design 2 proposed 1981-2011 / 2012-2017 / 2018-2024. I chose Design 1's because they are EarthquakeNPP's own frozen splits, which is what makes gate G4 (reproduce the published ETAS scores to <0.01 nats) possible at all — reproducibility must come before novelty. Design 2's longer sealed window is preserved as the RESERVE. Note explicitly and in advance: **Ridgecrest (July 2019) falls inside HELD-OUT and will dominate it.** That is deliberate — it is the most consequential sequence available — and the sequence jackknife (A2 below) is the pre-registered arbiter of whether any margin survives it.

REPLICATION (declared now, not chosen later): SCEDC M>=2.5 1981-2020; QTM SanJac and SaltonSea M>=1.0 2008-2017; WHITE M>=0.6 2008-2017; plus, after human fetch, Italy (HORUS / CSEP-Italy) and Japan (JMA unified hypocenter catalog — **NOT** EarthquakeNPP's `Japan_Deprecated`, which is ComCat-derived at modal magnitude 4.2 and is a trap for anyone who thinks they have Mc 1.0 regional data) and New Zealand (GeoNet full-history QuakeSearch).
SYNTHETIC TRUTH: EarthquakeNPP's ETAS_California catalog with known generating parameters, plus its deliberately magnitude-incomplete twin.
PROSPECTIVE ARM: the Shelter dual-clock feed, which lets us reconstruct the exact information state at any past instant. Conventional catalogs silently overwrite events with final reviewed parameters and cannot support honest pseudo-prospective evaluation.

--- METRICS ---
**PRIMARY:** information gain per earthquake (IGPE, nats) versus **B2** — the properly-configured ETAS — computed with pyCSEP's `paired_t_test` but with the parametric CI **replaced by a 10,000-resample block bootstrap over ETAS-declustered sequences**. Rationale stated explicitly: the CSEP t-test variance is mis-specified with non-uniform p-values under the null, and clustered targets make effective N far below nominal. The CSEP t-test and W-test are reported alongside as legacy, never as primary inference.

**MANDATORY SECONDARIES, reported whatever the outcome:**
- N-test in its **negative-binomial** form, not Poisson. Counts are over-dispersed; the classic Poisson N-test is low-power and the wrong likelihood.
- S-test on a **quadtree multi-resolution grid**, not a uniform 0.1-degree grid. On a fine uniform global grid the S-test has near-zero power to reject even a spatially uniform forecast with 651 events; quadtree geometry recovers power ~0.9 with as few as 8. Choosing the geometry badly makes the exercise unfalsifiable — and this is a solved discretisation artifact, not a law of nature. *(Contradiction resolved: Design 1 listed low S-test power as a near-binding ceiling; it is a binning pathology, and I rank it as a fixable harness decision, not a ceiling.)*
- M-test in resampled and MLL variants, with the standing note that the classic M-test is near-vacuous when every entrant assumes the same GR form with similar b — it checks that you used GR, not that you forecast anything.
- **Molchan trajectory and Area Skill Score as a second, likelihood-independent axis.** Log-scores are sensitive to the water level and to a handful of high-leverage cells; the alarm-based axis is not. **Where the two axes disagree, the disagreement IS the finding.**
- Brier score with full Murphy decomposition (reliability / resolution / uncertainty) and a reliability diagram.
- **The IGPE rate-calibration decomposition, always.** Rhoades IGPE is (SUM log-ratio - (N1-N2))/N; the second term is pure total-count calibration, not spatial or temporal information. In CSEP-Italy ~1.3 of the winning model's 8.13 nats accrued in windows containing ZERO earthquakes — credit for forecasting fewer events, reported as skill. Both terms reported separately every time.
- **Terminal metric: cost-loss expected net benefit** at C/L = 1e-4 (cheap preparedness), 1e-3 (marginal), 7e-3 (evacuation), with absolute probability printed beside every gain.
- **NEVER:** classification accuracy on "was there a larger event." A constant "no" scores 85-95% against the ~5-15% base rate. Any result reported as accuracy is void.
- **NEVER report a gain without naming its baseline, grid pitch, Mc and magnitude threshold.** Published gains in this literature span 0.1 bits to 9 nats for nominally similar models purely because of differing baselines. A lint rule checks that every "nats" string in the repo's prose sits within 80 characters of "over" or "vs".

--- MAGNITUDE STRATIFICATION (pre-registered) ---
Every result reported at M>=2.5 (calibration), M>=3.0, **M>=4.0 (the headline)**, M>=4.95 (hazard-adjacent) and M>=5.95 (reported, never headlined). Standing statement in every results table: *the threshold with statistical power is the one that does not matter, and the threshold that matters has no power.* A gain that exists only at M<=3.0 is reported as a **catalog-completeness result**, not a forecasting result, and is explicitly forbidden from being described as improved forecast skill.

--- POWER ANALYSIS, RUN BEFORE ANY CONFIRMATORY TEST ---
Measured targets in the 13.05-yr window: 21,889 (M>=2.5); 7,007 (3.0); 727 (4.0); 62 (4.95); 6 (5.95). Estimate sigma (per-event log-ratio dispersion) and N_eff (sequences, from the block structure) **empirically on DEV**, not by assumption. Compute MDE = 2.8*sigma/sqrt(N_eff) and publish it alongside the pre-registration, before opening held-out. At sigma ~ 1 nat: MDE ~0.06-0.07 at M>=2.5, ~0.21 at M>=4.0, ~0.56 at M>=4.95, against a plausible true effect <= 0.07 nats. **If MDE exceeds the largest effect we could honestly claim, the confirmatory test AT THAT THRESHOLD IS NOT RUN, and the result reads "underpowered — no conclusion," never "null."**

--- ADOPTION RULES: TWO TIERS (contradiction resolved) ---
Design 3's +0.50 nats / CI-lower-bound > +0.20 threshold and Designs 1-2's +0.05 nats threshold are not in conflict — they answer different questions and both are kept, explicitly labelled:

**TIER L (whole-layer adoption: does the short-term clustering layer earn a place at all?)** ADOPT iff IGPE over the frozen time-independent B1 map is **>= +0.50 nats/event with a 95% sequence-block-bootstrap lower bound > +0.20**, AND the negative-binomial N-test passes at 1-day and 7-day horizons, AND leave-one-sequence-out does not flip the sign. (Our own first-party measurement puts the true value at ~+4.3 nats at Mc 2.5, so this gate is expected to pass comfortably in California and is a genuine test only in sparse regions and at high magnitude.)

**TIER C (component adoption: does a challenger covariate earn a non-zero beta over B2?)** ADOPT iff, on DEV first and then confirmed **once** on HELD-OUT, ALL FIVE hold:
- **A1** Delta-IGPE vs B2 >= **+0.05 nats/event** at M>=3.95 AND >= **0.00** at M>=4.95 (sign consistency across thresholds), with the 95% sequence-block-bootstrap LOWER bound > 0.
- **A2 SEQUENCE JACKKNIFE:** removing the single highest-contributing sequence leaves the lower bound > 0. Direct defense against the CSEP-Italy failure mode (21 of 24 targets from two sequences, 74% of margin from one 2012 window) and the RELM El Mayor-Cucapah confound (one aftershock cloud near the region's southern boundary dominating a ~30-event target set).
- **A3 REPLICATION:** sign reproduces in >= 2 of 3 independent regions, each with an independently computed block-bootstrap CI, with >= 50% of the California magnitude. Single-region results are conventions, not findings.
- **A4 NO REGRESSION:** the component does not cause failure of any consistency test B2 passes, and does not degrade the magnitude test or the reliability diagram. Components are checked individually — an L-test can pass while its space-magnitude and rate components both perform poorly, so aggregate scores hide component failures.
- **A5 MAGNITUDE:** non-null at M>=4.0, or it is reclassified as a completeness/data-quality result.
Anything failing any condition is **REJECTED, LOGGED, AND RETIRED PERMANENTLY** — no re-tuning, no re-parameterising, no re-testing on the same holdout. We pre-commit to publishing rejections in a standing rejection ledger with each item's number, CI and alarm volume.

--- INTEGRITY MACHINERY (runs on every evaluation, gates every result) ---
- **G1 LEAK CANARY:** event-id-hash covariate and random-phase surrogate must both score 0 +/- bootstrap noise. Non-zero => pipeline leaks => run void, no interpretation of the other numbers.
- **G1b SPLITTER PROOF:** AMR with free radius/window must score |IGPE| < 0.05 nats under the grouped forward-in-time splitter AND > 0.30 nats under a naive random k-fold — demonstrating the splitter measurably removes a known leak.
- **G2 SYNTHETIC RECOVERY:** on the synthetic ETAS catalog with known parameters the harness must credit a challenger ~0 gain over the true generating model, and must surface the known bias on the deliberately incomplete twin. A harness that cannot recover a known truth cannot adjudicate an unknown one.
- **G3 IDENTITY:** paired_t_test of a forecast against a byte-identical copy must return exactly 0.
- **G4 REPRODUCTION:** reproduce EarthquakeNPP's published ETAS scores to <0.01 nats (ComCat: ETAS nll 7.2554, Poisson 13.2619, delta 6.006) on ComCat plus >= 2 other catalogs, and reproduce the CSEP-Italy 19-model pairwise IGPE matrix to 1e-3 nats, and the documented HKJ calibration discrepancy exactly (21.13 expected vs 31 observed M>=4.95, 2006-2010), before touching anything of our own.
- **G5 AFTERSHOCK/MAINSHOCK SEPARATION:** every reported result is tagged as aftershock-sequence forecasting or independent-mainshock forecasting, and the two are never aggregated. This is the field's most common conflation, and the shipped HKJ pair is the live example — the aftershock-inclusive variant won RELM and performs statistically *worse* than the mainshock variant in the following 2010-2015 window.
- **G6 WATER-LEVEL SENSITIVITY:** every headline IGPE reported at three water-level settings. If the ranking flips, the ranking is not real.
- **G7 SEED DISCIPLINE:** mean over seeds, never best-of-N. Best-of-3-seeds is an undisclosed selection rule.
- **G8 VINTAGE-CORRECT SCORING:** every forecast scored twice — against the catalog as of issue time and against the final catalog. Divergence between them is itself a finding.

--- ANTI-GAMING PROVISIONS (non-negotiable) ---
- **One holdout opening per stage.** Exceeding the cap is itself a stop condition; any re-test requires the RESERVE window and is labelled a second attempt in the results.
- **Multiplicity pre-declared.** Full feature list and test count frozen before Stage 3. FDR controlled at 0.05 across the feature family using Benjamini-Yekutieli (tests are dependent). Anything added after the freeze is exploratory and cannot be promoted this cycle.
- **Quarantine rule:** a speculative feature may never be promoted on retrospective evidence regardless of effect size. Promotion requires a registered forward-in-time window of >= 30 target events at M>=3.95 or >= 2 independent sequences, whichever is longer.
- **Alarm-volume rule:** any claim phrased as a *prediction* must report tau (space-time-magnitude alarm fraction) on a Molchan diagram alongside hit rate, or it is not reported. This single rule is what killed VAN, AMR and Haicheng-as-method, and it is the one the FTLS has never been subjected to.
- **Bug-hunt tripwire:** any Delta-IGPE > +0.15 nats at M>=4.0 is presumed a defect until it survives a checklist — rupture-grouped split verified, no covariate computed with post-event information, canary still zero, jackknife survived, replication attempted. The largest credible margin over ETAS ever reported is ~0.069 nats; a result twice that size is more likely a leak than a breakthrough.
- **Negative-control block appears in every results table.** Any Tier-C control showing skill VOIDS the cycle pending a leak audit. No exceptions, no explaining it away.
- **Calibration is a shipping gate, not a diagnostic.** A forecast reaches a user surface only if every populated probability decile's observed frequency is within a factor of 2 of predicted, aggregate observed/expected count ratio is in [0.7, 1.4], and the negative-binomial N-test passes at 1-day and 7-day horizons. Positive IGPE with bad calibration ships to the rejection ledger, not to users — because the surface asserts an *absolute* probability and information gain is indifferent to absolute calibration. This gate is recomputed in CI on every catalog refresh and auto-demotes a shipped surface that drifts out of it.

--- WHAT WE DO NOT GET TO DO ---
No retrospective-only skill claims (16 California multiplicative hybrids beat HKJ by +0.2 to +0.5 nats retrospectively and by ~0 prospectively — the field's most important methodological result, and a description of us, not of them). No comparing a tuned version of our model against an untuned baseline. No quoting a probability gain without the absolute probability and base rate beside it. No claiming operational value without the cost-loss calculation. No re-selecting thresholds, regions or magnitude bands after seeing held-out results. And nothing counts as falsification that isn't prospective, CI-bearing and sequence-bootstrapped: a 3% forecast followed by an earthquake is a calibrated model experiencing a 3% outcome (which is exactly why the Ridgecrest 2.8% anecdote is weak evidence in *both* directions), and a competitor's better retrospective number moves nothing.

### Stage 5 — The magnitude head: mainshock discrimination, the structural question (12 weeks; expected null, and that is the point)

**Success criterion (pre-registered).** Deliberately hard and pre-registered: the module must improve the LOG-SCORE of the binary 'a larger event follows' relative to BOTH a constant Bath-type ~5% climatology AND standard ETAS's GR tail by >= +0.02 nats per sequence, on a sealed set of >= 100 M>=5 sequences across >= 3 regions, with a Molchan trajectory significantly below the random diagonal at a reported alarm fraction tau. **Honest expectation.** WE EXPECT ZERO. This is the field's hard ceiling (Helmstetter & Sornette, conditional on magnitude-independence). The FTLS's prospective record is empty seven years on; its output is unstable across defensible analyst choices; a blind algorithm sharply degrades its discriminative power; the short-term-incompleteness bias points the SAME WAY as the claimed signal; and the static b-vs-stress relation (db/dsigma ~ -0.0012/MPa) is roughly two orders of magnitude too weak to produce transients of the claimed amplitude. The value of this stage is not a win — it is producing the first properly scored, prospective, alarm-volume-reported Molchan diagram for foreshock discrimination, which does not currently exist anywhere. A clean, well-powered null is a publishable, program-advancing result and we pre-commit to publishing it. ## 5. Stop rules

**STOP RULES — each a specific, checkable condition with a named consequence, written now so that hitting one is a scheduled event rather than a judgement call made by people who have become invested.**

**S0 — HARNESS GATE (Stage 0). Stop-and-fix.** If the leak canary or random-phase surrogate scores non-zero, or the AMR detector fails to show the >0.30 (naive k-fold) / <0.05 (grouped split) contrast, or synthetic recovery fails, or we cannot reproduce the published ETAS scores to <0.01 nats and the CSEP-Italy pairwise matrix to 1e-3 nats: no modelling proceeds. If not fixed within 4 weeks of focused work, stop the program. Every result produced before a canary failure — including any of our own already-reported numbers — is void until re-measured. This rule exists because the two most consequential documented failures in ML seismology were both leakage, and both were discovered by outsiders after publication.

**S1 — BASELINE GATE (Stage 1). Most likely early stop, and it produces a genuinely useful result.** If B2 (ETAS with a spatially-varying background) beats the shipped uniform-background ETAS by enough to close most of the gap the challenger was designed to fill, stop the "beat ETAS" track entirely. Redirect to Stage 2, Stage 5 and Stage 6, and report the corrected baseline as the deliverable: a harder, honest bar that the field has been scoring against a softer version of. We do not build on, or claim to beat, an ETAS we quietly weakened.

**S2 — THE "MORE DATA + BETTER STATISTICS" TRIPWIRE (Stage 2/3).** If the combined Tier A stack plus the Mc-lowering lever does not clear **+0.10 nats/event over the best reference ETAS at M>=4.95 on the sealed holdout**, the maximum-signal premise has failed on its most favourable ground — the cheapest, best-cited, most mechanistically defensible refinements applied to the densest catalog obtainable without a human. Stop adding Tier A features. This is the specific stop rule that answers the user's premise on its own terms.

**S3 — NULL GATE (Stage 3). The main falsification.** If, on the sealed holdout, the entire residual layer returns an aggregate IGPE whose **95% CI upper bound is below +0.05 nats/event at M>=4.95**, AND every covariate individually has a 95% block-bootstrap lower bound <= 0 on DEV: stop adding covariates. Do not tune. Do not try a bigger network. Do not sweep another radius. Write up the null and publish it. The specifically prohibited move is what killed AMR — per-event optimisation of radius and time window manufactures skill from noise, and the same optimisation recovers *decelerating* moment release from the same data.

**S4 — MAGNITUDE GATE (every stage).** If the only non-null result lives at M<=3.0, we stop calling it forecast skill. It is reported as a catalog-completeness or data-quality result. Continuing to describe it as improved forecasting would repeat the field's central rhetorical error: all the headline CSEP skill sits at M>=3.95, and inter-model gains go to zero by M>=5, which means the entire measured skill record of the class sits at magnitudes with no hazard consequence.

**S5 — REPLICATION GATE (Stage 4). Permanent retirement, no appeal.** A feature promoted in California that fails to replicate with the same sign in both a second and a third region is retired. Not re-tuned, not re-parameterised, not re-tested on a different window. Ranking instability across evaluation periods is established, not contested, and single-region promotion is how it gets laundered into a finding.

**S6 — SEQUENCE JACKKNIFE GATE (every stage). The most likely specific failure mode.** If removing the single largest-contributing sequence kills the margin, stop and report the result as "one sequence, not a model." Precedent is precise: 21 of 24 CSEP-Italy decade targets from two aftershock sequences, five of ten years with zero targets, 74% of the winning model's cumulative margin from one 2012 window, and the RELM target set dominated by El Mayor-Cucapah. Ridgecrest sits inside our own held-out and will test this directly.

**S7 — POWER GATE (before any confirmatory test).** If the empirically-measured MDE exceeds the largest effect we could honestly claim (~0.07 nats), do not run the confirmatory test at that threshold. Publish the MDE and report "underpowered — no conclusion," never a null as evidence of absence. Then either accept the question is undecidable with available targets or go get more regions and longer registered windows — the only two things that fix power. At M>=4.95 in one region this condition will fire more often than not, and admitting that is part of the deliverable.

**S8 — TRIPWIRE INVERSION.** Any Delta-IGPE above +0.15 nats at M>=4.0 triggers a bug hunt, not a celebration. The largest credible margin over ETAS ever reported is ~0.069 nats, unreplicated and never CSEP-registered. A result twice that size is more likely a leak than a discovery and does not get written up until it survives the full checklist and replicates.

**S9 — PROTOCOL-INTEGRITY FAILURE.** Exceeding one sealed-holdout opening per stage, adding a feature after the registry freeze and promoting it in the same cycle, or reporting a gain without its baseline / grid pitch / Mc / magnitude threshold, is itself a stop condition. Two retired windows in one stage ends the stage. The program's only real asset is that its numbers are trustworthy; a protocol breach destroys more value than any feature could add.

**S10 — COMMUNICATION FAILURE (Stage 6).** If comprehension testing cannot reach >= 80% of lay readers restating the absolute probability or its complement, with < 10% expressing a deterministic expectation, after three design iterations, the elevated-state surface does not ship in any form. A number most readers convert into "an earthquake is coming" is not a forecast communicated badly; it is a *prediction being made by the interface*, and this program does not make predictions. Same for rarity: above 5% of place-days elevated, or 2% above 10x gain, the state stops being information and becomes ambient anxiety, and the 999:1 false-alarm arithmetic becomes an ethical liability rather than a disclosed limitation.

**S11 — PRODUCT FAILURE (Stage 6). The terminal stop.** If no configuration produces positive cost-loss net benefit at any C/L ratio anyone would act on, outside the post-mainshock regime already served by existing USGS/GNS/INGV aftershock products, the program has no product and should end. Reproducing the field's existing condition — genuine statistical skill with zero demonstrated decision utility — is not a contribution.

**S12 — GLOBAL STOP.** Eighteen months with no component clearing the full five-condition adoption rule: the premise is wrong for this data and this team. The honest deliverable is the harness, the corrected baseline ladder, the rejection ledger, the decision layer, the first properly-scored prospective Molchan diagram for foreshock discrimination, and the first cost-loss evaluation of operational earthquake forecasting. That is a real contribution — the field's single most reproducible finding is that retrospective gains do not survive prospective testing, and a well-documented negative is worth more than another unreplicated positive.

--- WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THE BROADER PREMISE, AS DISTINCT FROM FALSIFYING US ---
Keep these separate. Our model failing is evidence about our model. "Given enough data we can predict earthquakes" is a different claim with a different test.

It would be **SUPPORTED** by: a registered, prospective, sequence-jackknife-robust demonstration of a sustained probability gain above ~1,000x at M>=5, at a specific place and time, OUTSIDE the post-mainshock window, replicated in a second region, scored against a properly-configured ETAS rather than a uniform-Poisson strawman, with the alarm fraction tau reported on a Molchan diagram. Nothing in a century has come within an order of magnitude of that, and the one place gains of that size do occur — hours to days after a nearby large event — is delivered by Omori-Utsu statistics from the 1980s and tells you nothing about anticipating an independent mainshock. The narrower, genuinely reachable version is the Stage 5 criterion: a history-dependent magnitude model beating both the Bath climatology and the ETAS GR tail by >= 0.02 nats/sequence across >= 3 regions with a Molchan trajectory significantly below the diagonal. That would be the first real breach of the structural ceiling, and it is the only outcome in this program that would change the answer to the premise rather than sharpen it.

It would be **FALSIFIED AS A DATA-VOLUME CLAIM** — and in my view already is — by the arithmetic in the verdict: 13.05 years of the densest instrumentation on Earth produced 62 targets at M>=4.95 and 6 at M>=5.95, and the MDE at those counts (~0.56 nats) exceeds the largest credible effect (~0.07 nats) by roughly 8x. Template matching already delivered the 10x catalog densification the premise asks for (southern California, ~180k -> 1.81M events, Mc 1.7 -> 0.3) and no precursor emerged from it. More sensors produce more small events, not more large-earthquake targets. The constraint is the Gutenberg-Richter tail and the calendar, not the disk. The single caveat that keeps the premise alive in a narrow sense is Stage 1's Mc ablation: more data does buy more *training* events even when it buys no *targets*, and that lever has never been cleanly tested.

--- WHAT DOES NOT COUNT AS FALSIFICATION, STATED NOW SO IT CANNOT BE INVOKED LATER ---
A low-probability event occurring inside an elevated window (a 2.8% forecast followed by an earthquake is a calibrated model experiencing a 2.8% outcome — which is why the Ridgecrest anecdote is weak evidence in *both* directions, and why that evaluation was pseudo-prospective anyway). A single sequence dominating a margin in either direction (that is what leave-one-sequence-out is for). A competitor publishing a better retrospective number. Only prospective, CI-bearing, sequence-bootstrapped results on untouched windows move any decision here.

--- THE MOST LIKELY HONEST OUTCOME, COMMITTED IN ADVANCE SO IT CANNOT BE REFRAMED LATER ---
We reproduce ETAS. We fix the baseline and find it was mis-specified in the benchmark we inherited, which raises the bar for everyone including us. The Mc-lowering ablation is a coin flip and closes a 20-year-old open question either way. We get a small, real, magnitude-bounded gain from incompleteness correction and a number-distribution upgrade, and we correctly label it a data-quality improvement rather than forecast skill. The residual correction layer returns approximately zero at M>=4.0. The magnitude head returns a clean null with the first Molchan diagram anyone has drawn for it. We publish the null, the harness, the corrected baseline ladder, the rejection ledger, and a decision layer that always shows absolute probability next to probability gain. Total elapsed: 12-18 months.

That outcome is worth building. What is not worth building is the version where we tune until something crosses p<0.05 on 62 clustered events using a test whose variance is known to be wrong, and call it a breakthrough. The seal, the canaries, the jackknife, the block bootstrap, the replication requirement, the FDR control and the power gate exist to make that version mechanically unavailable to us.
